PAYLOAD – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026
Applies to: the mobile game PAYLOAD (package com.pinkmanstudio.payload) published on Google Play by Pinkman Studio.

This page explains, in plain language, what information is used when you play PAYLOAD, who handles it, and the choices you have. The short version:

  • We (Pinkman Studio) do not run any servers for this game. No accounts, no login, no analytics, no chat.
  • Multiplayer is optional. On the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, phones talk to each other directly. Online races and Quick Match go through Unity Gaming Services (Relay, Lobby, Leaderboards), which use an anonymous player ID and your IP address to connect the players. Other racers see your nickname, rocket skin, multiplayer level and your rocket during the race; the online leaderboard shows your nickname and points to everyone.
  • Community maps are optional. Maps you publish (with their name, your nickname and your country code) are stored in Unity Cloud Save and shown to every player; plays, votes and reports are counted with your anonymous player ID.
  • Your progress, settings and unlocked skins are saved on your device.
  • The free version shows ads using Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads (details below). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • The optional Premium purchase (no ads, premium skins and cosmetic extras) is processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Premium players do not get ads, and the game no longer starts the advertising SDK for them.

2. Information stored only on your device

PAYLOAD saves the following on your phone or tablet so the game works:

  • game settings (for example control mode, sensitivity, sound, music and vibration);
  • progress (levels completed, medals, best times and scores, stars, achievements and lifetime statistics such as the number of flights and crashes; the crash count and play time are also used on your device to decide when an ad break may be shown);
  • the skins and cosmetic extras you have unlocked and selected;
  • whether you own Premium (a local flag, confirmed again with Google Play when the game starts);
  • your ad-consent choices, stored on your device by Google's consent tool (see section 3).

We do not receive this information. It stays in the game's private storage on your device, unless you turn on cloud save (section 5a).

  • Deleting it: uninstalling PAYLOAD, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → PAYLOAD → Storage, deletes it from the device.
  • Android backup and transfer: if you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include this save data in your device backup, stored by Google in your Google account, and restore it if you reinstall the game; Android may also copy it when you move to a new phone. We cannot access these backups. You control them in your Android backup settings.

If you buy Premium, it can be restored on a new device through Google Play, because Google keeps the record of your purchase.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

PAYLOAD is free and is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location, such as your country or city – not your precise GPS location);
  • app interactions (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time, hang rate and energy usage);
  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google and its partners may also place and read cookies or similar technologies on your device, and use web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers, to collect information as a result of serving ads in the game. This data is encrypted in transit. Google decides how it uses this data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports (for example the total number of ads shown and earnings).

  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Personalised, non-personalised and limited ads

  • Personalised ads are chosen using information about your past activity.
  • Non-personalised ads are not targeted using your past activity. They are chosen from context (for example the app and your general location) and still use cookies or device identifiers to limit how often you see an ad, for aggregated reporting and for fraud prevention.
  • Limited ads may be shown if you have not given consent to use device storage: they turn off all personalisation and features that need a device identifier.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform) asks for your choices before personalised ads are used. The message lists Google and the other ad partners involved. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the game. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

US state privacy rights. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you may see a message that lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or of "targeted advertising". You can also use Settings → Privacy options when it is shown, or the device controls below. When you opt out, AdMob does not serve ads based on your prior behaviour.

Device controls (everyone):

  • Most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (on some devices: Settings → Google → Ads). After deletion, apps receive only zeros instead of an ID.
  • Some older Android versions: Settings → Privacy → Advanced → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • Google ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com

When ads appear. Ads are only shown at a break: after a failed run, when you restart a level, or when you go on to the next level. Never during a flight, never in multiplayer, and never more often than the game's built-in limits allow.

Premium players. If you buy Premium, the game stops showing ads and no longer starts the Google Mobile Ads SDK, so the advertising data above is no longer collected while you play.

4. Multiplayer races (Wi-Fi and online)

Multiplayer is optional: nothing below happens unless you open MODES → MULTIPLAYER and host or join a race.

What other players see. To race together, the game sends the other players in your race: the nickname you type (it does not have to be your real name), your rocket skin and cosmetic extras, your READY state, and during a race your rocket's position, direction and state (for example flying, crashed or finished) about 20 times a second, plus your finishing time. It is used only to show your rocket and results on their screens and is not stored after the race. There is no chat.

Wi-Fi / hotspot races. Phones on the same local network connect to each other directly. To find games, the game sends a short "is anyone hosting?" message to the local network and hosts answer with their nickname, the chosen level and the number of players. This data stays on your local network; it does not go to us or to any server.

Online races (Unity Relay). Online races use Unity Relay and Unity Authentication (and, for Quick Match and leaderboards, Unity Lobby and Unity Leaderboards), services of Unity Technologies ("Unity", part of Unity Gaming Services), because phones on different networks cannot reach each other directly:

  • When you host or join an online race, the game signs in to Unity Authentication anonymously: no email, password or account is needed. Unity creates a random player ID for this installation (stored on your device so it stays the same next time).
  • Unity Relay receives your IP address and that player ID to connect you, and forwards the race data described above between the players in your room. The room is joined with a 6-character code the host shares.
  • Unity acts as our processor (under the GDPR) and service provider (under US state privacy laws): it handles this data only to provide the service to us. Unity keeps Relay's IP address and player ID records for 30 days; the anonymous player ID itself is kept until it is deleted (see section 14). Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Quick Match (Unity Lobby). QUICK MATCH finds random players: the game asks Unity Lobby for an open public room with the same game version, or opens one that others can find. Unity Lobby receives your IP address and anonymous player ID; it does not store them, but keeps the player ID in its logs for up to 30 days. The room only holds the connection code and game version, not your nickname.

Leaderboards (Unity Leaderboards). Quick Match races give leaderboard points. Your points, your anonymous player ID and your nickname (stored as your Unity player name) are saved in Unity Leaderboards and the nickname and points are shown to all players on the global board and to players you have raced on their "players you've raced" board. Pick a nickname that is not your real name if you prefer. The list of players you have raced is kept only on your device. Unity Leaderboards also receives your IP address. Wi-Fi and code rooms never send anything to the leaderboard.

Multiplayer level. Your multiplayer XP and level are saved on your device (section 2) and your level is shown to the players in your room.

Local network permission. To find Wi-Fi races, the game needs Android's Wi-Fi state and multicast permissions (install-time permissions, no prompt). On newer Android versions you may be asked to allow access to nearby devices / the local network; Wi-Fi races cannot work without it, everything else does.

5. Community maps (build, share and play player-made levels)

Community maps are optional: nothing below happens unless you open COMMUNITY.

  • Building and testing a map happens on your device. Drafts are saved in the game's private storage (section 2) and are not sent anywhere until you publish them.
  • Publishing. When you publish a map, the game sends it to Unity Cloud Code and Unity Cloud Save (Unity Gaming Services), signed in with the same anonymous Unity player ID as online races (section 4). Stored with the map: the map itself (its pieces and settings), the map name, your multiplayer nickname, a two-letter country or region code taken from your device's region setting (not your location), your anonymous player ID (so only you can update or delete it), the time it took you to beat it, when it was published and updated, and the numbers of votes, plays and wins. Everyone who opens COMMUNITY can see the map, its name, your nickname and the country code. Please do not put personal information in map names.
  • Playing, voting and reporting. When you play a published map, the game downloads it and records, with your anonymous player ID, that you played (and beat) it, to count plays once per player and to let you vote once per map. Your vote is stored with your player ID; other players only see the totals. If you report a map, the report is stored with your player ID (each player counts once) and the reason, and is used for moderation: enough reports hide a map automatically, and we review hidden maps in the Unity Dashboard.
  • On your device only: the maps you saved (bookmarks), the creators you blocked, and whether you accepted the community rules.
  • Share links and codes. Sharing a map shares its 6-character code and a link to our website (https://erblinkrasniqi.github.io). That page is static and does not collect anything itself; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log the IP address of visitors for security (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  • Unity acts as our processor / service provider for this data, as in section 4.

5a. Cloud save (optional, Google Play Games sign-in)

Cloud save is off until you tap SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE in Settings → GAME.

  • Signing in. The game asks Google Play Games to sign you in. Google then gives the game a one-time code that Unity Authentication uses to link your Google Play Games account to your anonymous Unity player ID (section 4). We receive your Play Games player identifier through Unity; we do not receive your email address, your Google account name or your contacts, and your Google name is not shown in the game (you keep your own nickname).
  • What is stored. A copy of your save (section 2: progress, unlocked items, lifetime statistics, multiplayer XP, settings and your nickname) is kept in Unity Cloud Save with your Unity player ID, and updated a few seconds after the game saves. When you reinstall PAYLOAD or use a new phone and Google Play Games signs you in, the game finds your linked Unity player again and restores your progress, your published community maps and your leaderboard entry. If two devices have different progress, the game keeps the best of both.
  • Turning it off and deleting it. Settings → GAME → DELETE ONLINE ID deletes your Unity player, which deletes the cloud copy and the link to your Google Play Games account. Your save on the device stays. You can also manage Google Play Games itself in the Play Games app or your Google account settings.
  • Google Play Games processes your sign-in under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Unity acts as our processor for the cloud copy, as in section 4.

6. In-app purchase – Google Play Billing

The optional Premium purchase is processed entirely by Google Play.

  • Google collects and processes your payment details (for example card or bank information). We never receive them.
  • When you buy, Google Play tells the game the purchase is valid, using a purchase confirmation that contains an order ID, a purchase token, the product bought and the purchase time. The game uses this on your device to unlock Premium. We do not run a server, and the game does not send this information to us.
  • Google gives us, as the seller, limited transaction records in our Google Play Console: for example the order ID, product, price, date, taxes, refund status and the buyer's country (and sometimes state or region, city or postal code). Google also lets sellers look up an order by the buyer's full email address, which we only do if you give it to us (for example in a support request). We use this only for accounting, tax, refunds, customer support and preventing fraud.

Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice

Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) and applicable law; you can also contact us.

8. What we do NOT do

  • no user accounts, passwords or login (online races use only an anonymous Unity player ID, see section 4);
  • no analytics or tracking SDKs of our own;
  • no precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access;
  • we do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that legally or similarly significantly affect you.

Permissions the game uses: internet and network-state access (to load ads, talk to Google Play and play online races), Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast (to find Wi-Fi races on your local network; on Android versions that ask for it, local network / nearby devices access), vibration (for haptic feedback), the advertising-ID permission and related Android ad-services permissions (added automatically by the Google ads SDK), and the Google Play billing permission (for the Premium purchase).

13. Security

Google's SDKs encrypt the data they send (TLS). Online races are encrypted between your device and Unity Relay (DTLS). Save data stays in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We protect our developer and email accounts with strong passwords and 2-step verification and limit access to ourselves. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

14. Your rights and choices

Everyone can:

  • change ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (when shown), delete or reset the advertising ID, and use Google's ad settings (section 3);
  • delete all local save data by uninstalling or clearing the game's storage;
  • ask us to remove your leaderboard entry (email us your nickname; we delete it in the Unity Dashboard);
  • delete your anonymous online player ID with Settings → GAME → DELETE ONLINE ID (it first deletes every community map you published and your plays and votes, then asks Unity to delete the ID; a new one is created only if you play online again), or by emailing us;
  • unpublish any of your community maps at any time in COMMUNITY → MY MAPS;
  • email us at pinkwwman@gmail.com with any question or request.

EEA and UK residents have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of their personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Residents of US states with privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others) may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. When personalised ads are on, the Google ads SDK's data collection may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some of these laws; you can opt out as described in section 3. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. (Premium is an ordinary paid product, not a reward for sharing data.)

  • Categories of personal information involved (last 12 months): identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, anonymous Unity player ID for online races); the nickname you choose for multiplayer; internet or other electronic activity (interactions with ads and the app); approximate geolocation derived from IP address; commercial information (purchase records provided by Google Play); and, if you email us, your email address and messages.
  • Sources: your device (through the Google and Unity SDKs), Google Play, and you.
  • Purposes: see sections 3–7.

How to make a request: email pinkwwman@gmail.com. Because we do not have accounts, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. To find purchase records, please include your Google Play order ID (starting with "GPA."). Advertising data is held by Google, not by us; use Google's tools above, and we will help where we can. We may ask for information to confirm a request is genuine. We reply within one month (EEA/UK) or 45 days (US), and tell you if we need more time where the law allows.

Complaints. You can complain to us directly at pinkwwman@gmail.com.
